Security posture

Ownership is an architectural decision.

Credentials, sessions, content, and operational state are designed to stay within the installation the customer controls.

◈
Local owner authentication

Password verification and sessions remain inside the deployed Worker and D1 database.

◈
Encrypted Meta credentials

Access tokens and app secrets are encrypted before persistence in customer-owned storage.

◈
Signed webhook ingress

Incoming Meta events are authenticated and safely queued before background processing.

◈
Explicit data controls

Export and destructive deletion remain authenticated, deliberate installation-owner actions.
