Simple by design

From click to a platform you can explore.

The product flow belongs in the dashboard, not in a terminal. Customers deploy, create their owner account, and can inspect the full product before connecting their production number.

01 / DEPLOY

Choose your account

Keep Cloudflare’s preselected resources and click deploy. There are no OpenWA secrets or Meta IDs to enter on the deployment screen.

02 / EXPLORE

Start in a sample workspace

Immediately browse sample conversations, messages, contacts, templates, API access, activity, and data controls. Demo actions are safe and never call Meta.

03 / CONNECT

Go live when ready

Connect Meta from persistent WhatsApp settings. Once connected, choose to keep exploring or remove supplied samples for a clean production workspace.

Data sovereignty, without the slogan

Your messages do not pass through our servers.

OpenWA CORE is self-deployed. The deployed Worker communicates with Meta directly and stores operational data in the customer’s Cloudflare resources.

CORE

Open-source messaging infrastructure: signed webhooks, outbound dispatch, contact/conversation storage, templates, and a customer-owned dashboard.

customer Cloudflare → Meta

PREMIUM, later

Optional managed workflow, CRM, team inbox, integrations, and AI capabilities can extend the customer-owned core without becoming a message proxy.
